This updates Stacks to a newer build.

It replaces the Audio Unit, the VST3 and the Stacks app. Your saved presets and settings stay as they are, and the sound engine data installed earlier is reused.

If Stacks has never been installed on this Mac, use the full installer instead: this update does not include the sound engine data.

After installing, restart your DAW (Logic Pro: Plug-in Manager, then Reset & Rescan Selection).
