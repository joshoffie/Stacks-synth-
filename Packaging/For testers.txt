STACKS - BETA NOTES

Thanks for testing. This is the first build to leave the studio, so anything
odd is worth telling us about.

NEEDS
A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 12 or later, 8 GB of memory
(16 GB is better), about 3 GB of free disk space.

INSTALL
1. Download Stacks-0.1.2.pkg (2.3 GB; the sound engine data is most of it).
   Already have Stacks? Download Stacks-0.1.2-update.pkg (small) instead.
2. Double-click it and click through. It installs the Audio Unit, the VST3,
   the Stacks app and the engine data. It asks for your Mac password once.
3. Open your DAW. Logic Pro and GarageBand: if Stacks doesn't appear under
   Hoffman Audio, open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, select Stacks
   and press "Reset & Rescan Selection", or just restart Logic.
   Ableton, Reaper, Bitwig, FL: rescan plug-ins or restart the DAW.
   The Stacks app in Applications works on its own, no DAW needed.
   Not showing up in Logic? Make sure Logic isn't running under Rosetta:
   Applications > Logic Pro > Cmd+I > untick "Open using Rosetta".
   Stacks only loads in a DAW running natively on Apple silicon.

FIRST SOUND
The first time you open Stacks, a short guide points out what to click on
each screen. You can bring it back from the gear menu.

Pick a kind of sound on the Home screen (Pad, Keys, Bass...). The very first
sound can take up to a minute while the engine warms up, and all five about
two minutes. After that it's quicker. You can click a block to hear it as
soon as it lands, even while the rest are still growing. Then use "Grow from
this one", "Save preset" or "Open the synth" for every knob.

NEW IN 0.1.2 (from your notes)
- The five sounds now land in a family tree. Drag the empty space to look
  back at every earlier generation and click any block to play it again.
- The + on a block adds it to the mix. Add two or three and Grow makes five
  sounds that take something from each.
- Close, Medium and Far are different now. Far designs new sounds around what
  made the original itself instead of nudging its knobs. Tell us if Far feels
  like new ideas that are still related.
- Life lets the sound slowly wander as you play. Click Life in the header
  for Off to 5 and a line on what it does; it's on the Home screen too, and
  it saves with the preset. Off is the preset exactly.
- The window scales. Drag a corner, or pick a size in the gear menu.
- Stop ends a batch early. Esc or the cross closes the guide.
- The Synth page has MACROS and ALL. Next to them, Sections picks which
  parts of ALL show; the ones left on grow to fill the space.
- While a sound grows, the knobs on the Synth page travel to each setting as
  the engine writes it, so you can watch it being built.
- A saved preset carries its tree. Open it later and every run that led to it
  is back. Picking a new kind of sound on Home starts a fresh tree.
- While blocks are in the mix, a line under the tree says which ones and
  what Grow will do with them.
