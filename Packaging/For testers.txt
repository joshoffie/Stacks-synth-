STACKS - BETA NOTES

Thanks for testing. This is the first build to leave the studio, so anything
odd is worth telling us about.

NEEDS
A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 12 or later, 8 GB of memory
(16 GB is better), about 3 GB of free disk space.

INSTALL
1. Download Stacks-0.1.1.pkg (2.3 GB; the sound engine data is most of it).
2. Double-click it and click through. It installs the Audio Unit, the VST3,
   the Stacks app and the engine data. It asks for your Mac password once.
3. Open your DAW. Logic Pro and GarageBand: if Stacks doesn't appear under
   Hoffman Audio, open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, select Stacks
   and press "Reset & Rescan Selection", or just restart Logic.
   Ableton, Reaper, Bitwig, FL: rescan plug-ins or restart the DAW.
   The Stacks app in Applications works on its own, no DAW needed.

FIRST SOUND
Pick a kind of sound on the Home screen (Pad, Keys, Bass...). The first one
after a launch takes 20 to 40 seconds while the engine warms up, longer the
very first time. After that it's quicker. You get five sounds: click a block
to hear it, play the keyboard, then "Grow from this one", "Save preset" or
"Open the synth" for every knob.

PLEASE TELL US
- Did the installer open with a double-click, with no warning beyond the
  password prompt?
- Did Stacks show up in your DAW? Which DAW and which macOS version?
- How long did the first sound take? Did the Home screen show anything while
  you waited?
- Anything that froze, crashed, went silent, or made a horrible noise.
- Anything confusing. If you didn't know what to do next, that's a bug too.

A screenshot plus what you clicked just before is the most useful thing you
can send.

Saved presets live in Music > Stacks Patches. Updates will come as a small
Stacks-x.x.x-update.pkg: same double-click, installs over this one.
